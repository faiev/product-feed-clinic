Who this is for

Primary customer: independent Google Ads / PPC agencies and freelance performance marketers who manage Shopping campaigns for multiple ecommerce clients but lack a dedicated feed specialist.

They can use this as a scoped back-office QA step before changing a client feed.

Not intended for: general consumers, job seekers, or merchants expecting guaranteed approval, account reinstatement or campaign management.

What the agency receives

  1. Prioritized findings with affected item IDs and row numbers.
  2. Corrected CSV/TSV for changes supported by source evidence.
  3. Change log with old value, new value and reason.
  4. Exceptions list for missing or conflicting facts.
  5. Short hand-off report describing limits and next steps.
Important limitations: A feed-only review cannot confirm account-level policy status, suspension status, shipping/tax configuration, crawlability, or price and availability consistency at live checkout. Google makes final eligibility decisions. No approval, reinstatement or sales outcome is guaranteed. Never send passwords, customer/order exports or payment data.
